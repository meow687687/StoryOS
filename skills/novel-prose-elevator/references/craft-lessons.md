# Craft Micro-Lessons

Every `[NEEDS IMAGE]` or `[UNCLEAR]` flag ships with one lesson: 2 sentences of craft + one tiny exercise. Match lesson depth to `voice.md` calibration (new writers get the full version; experienced get the one-line form).

## Format

```md
[NEEDS IMAGE — author to supply: "she was angry" (para 3)]
> Lesson (telling vs showing): Name the abstraction, then replace it with one concrete thing a camera could catch — a hand, a sound, a stopped motion.
> Exercise: reply with one sentence: what do her hands do?
```

## Library (one-line forms for experienced writers in parens)

1. **Telling vs showing** (Show one camera-catchable thing.) — abstraction → hand/sound/motion.
2. **Filter verbs** (Drop the lens; keep the object.) — "she saw the door open" → "the door opened".
3. **Dialogue tags** (Said + beat beats adverb.) — replace "he said angrily" with `said` + one action line.
4. **Repetition** (One good detail, once.) — same adjective twice in a paragraph → cut or upgrade the second.
5. **Abstract emotion** (Locate it in the body or room.) — "he felt sad" → where does the writer feel it? hands, throat, posture.
6. **Vague setting** (One true detail anchors a room.) — add a single specific object/sound/smell from the author's world, never invented by AI.
7. **Unclear antecedent** (Name the noun.) — "he gave it to him" → who, what, to whom.

Rules: lessons teach, never demonstrate with invented story text — examples stay generic ("a hand", "a door"), never Kael-specific. The writer's reply becomes a ledger entry; then re-run the elevator on that paragraph.
