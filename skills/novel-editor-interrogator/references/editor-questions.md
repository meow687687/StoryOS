# Editor Question Bank

Ask in this priority. Skip categories with no gap.

1. **Causation** — What makes beat N+1 follow beat N? Name the decision, accident, or revelation. No bridge = BLOCKING.
2. **Want vs Need** — What does the POV character want here, and what do they actually need? If identical, the scene has no tension.
3. **Stakes** — What is lost/changed if the scene goal fails? "Nothing" means cut or combine.
4. **Knowledge timing** — What does the reader know at this beat vs the character? Flag accidental dramatic-irony breaks.
5. **Consequence** — If this beat is cut entirely, what breaks downstream? If nothing, mark CANDIDATE-CUT.
6. **Consistency** — Does any action violate bible (traits, rules, geography, timeline)? Quote both sides; author resolves.
7. **POV/Tense** — Whose head, what tense? One shift per chapter max unless author declares otherwise.
8. **Scene goal** — Each scene: whose goal, what obstacle, what outcome (yes / no / yes-but / no-and)? Beats without outcomes are summary, not scene.
