# Bible Schema

Copy this shape. Delete nothing — mark `UNKNOWN` instead.

```md
# Story Bible: <TITLE>

## Premise
<1–3 sentences, author's words compressed> (dump §N)

## Characters
### <Name>
- Role: <as stated>
- Wants: <as stated | UNKNOWN>
- Wound/Ghost: <as stated | UNKNOWN>
- Voice note: <author's phrasing sample>
- Source: (dump §N)

## World Rules
- Rule: <stated rule> (dump §N)
- Cost/Limit: <as stated | UNKNOWN>

## Geography / Setting
- <Place>: <as stated> (dump §N)

## Timeline (stated order only)
1. <event> (dump §N)

## Themes / Motifs
- <as stated, else omit section>

## Explicit Non-Goals (author's inventions stay author's)
- Agent must not invent: ...
```

Rules:

- One claim per bullet. No compound claims.
- Every bullet ends with its ledger ID once logged: `(D7)`. Pre-ledger drafts may use `(dump §N)`; convert to D-IDs when the interrogator locks answers.
- Adjectives from the dump stay quoted: `"cunning"` not cunning (paraphrased).
- Contradictions in the dump are preserved side-by-side and flagged `CONTRADICTION — author to resolve`, never merged.
